# Candidate Scoring

Score only after research is complete.

- Catalyst quality and verification: 0–25
- Trend and relative strength: 0–20
- Volume, spread, and liquidity quality: 0–15
- Entry quality and definable downside: 0–15
- Reward-to-risk quality: 0–15
- Data freshness and confidence: 0–10

Default passing threshold: 78.

## Score discipline

- Use integer scores.
- Explain deductions.
- A passing score means eligible for risk review, not “buy.”
- Reject regardless of score when a hard risk rule fails.
- Do not inflate scores to fill an empty candidate list.
- Compare candidates using the same time horizon and evidence standard.
