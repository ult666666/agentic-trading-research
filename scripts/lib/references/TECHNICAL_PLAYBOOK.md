# Technical Playbook

Technical evidence helps define execution and risk; it does not prove future direction.

## Favor

- Clear trend structure.
- Relative strength versus SPY and QQQ.
- Confirming volume.
- Tight, orderly consolidation.
- Pullback to a defensible level.
- Breakout with room before obvious resistance.
- Entry close enough to invalidation to support at least 2:1 reward-to-risk.

## Avoid

- Chasing a vertical move.
- Entries far above support.
- Illiquid securities or wide spreads.
- Stops placed inside normal noise merely to make sizing work.
- Targets chosen only to force a 2:1 ratio.
- A pattern without a catalyst or market context.
- Using indicators without timestamps and underlying price data.

## Plan fields

Every setup needs:

- Time horizon.
- Entry zone.
- Invalidation level and reason.
- First target and reason.
- Expected reward-to-risk.
- Conditions that cancel the setup before entry.
- Conditions that require reassessment after entry.
